How to Play Chess — Beginner's Guide

🎯 Goal

Checkmate the computer's king so it cannot escape.

♙ Pawn

Moves forward one square, two from its starting square, and captures diagonally.

♘ Knight

Moves in an L shape and can jump over pieces.

♗ Bishop

Moves diagonally.

♖ Rook

Moves horizontally or vertically.

♕ Queen

Moves horizontally, vertically and diagonally.

♔ King

Moves one square in any direction.

🏰 Castling

A special king and rook move.

👑 Promotion

A pawn reaching the opposite end can become a queen, rook, bishop or knight.
